Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Walloria handles app data, permissions, diagnostics, purchases, and support requests.

Effective date

May 26, 2026

Data we collect and process

Walloria may process app settings, favorites, recent and local app data, purchase entitlement status, diagnostics, crash, performance, and analytics data, device and app information, cache and download activity, notification preferences, and feedback emails you send.

Permissions

Photos or Gallery access is used to save wallpapers only when you request that action. Notification permission is used for reminders only if you enable reminders. You can change permissions in your device settings.

How we use data

We use data to operate app features, provide and restore Premium access, improve reliability and performance, diagnose crashes, respond to feedback, protect the app, and comply with store and legal obligations.

Sharing

Service providers such as Firebase, payment processors, and email or share apps may process data to support app functionality, diagnostics, purchases, and communication. Walloria team does not sell personal data.

Retention and choices

Local data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the app, or uninstall Walloria. Store purchase records are managed by your purchase provider. You can manage permissions in system settings and subscriptions in your store account subscription settings.

Children

Walloria is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required in your location. If you believe a child provided personal data, contact us so we can review the request.

International processing

Your information may be processed in locations where Walloria team or service providers operate. Data protection rules may vary by location.

Updates and contact

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. For privacy questions, contact Walloria team at willsontat@gmail.com.
